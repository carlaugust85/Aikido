REFLECTION was birthed from the realization that modern sound has become too comfortable. We have traded the jagged edges of truth for the smooth veneers of digital perfection.

The project began in a basement where the humidity felt like a second skin. Every recorded note was a confrontation. Every silence was a threat. We are not making music; we are documenting the dissolution of the ego through sonic pressure.
